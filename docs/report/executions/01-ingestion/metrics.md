# 01 · Ingestion concurrency — Metrics

Frozen with the Plan, before the first point. Refs are cited from outside as
`01-ingestion/M6`.

Two source families with different lifetimes, gating different things.

Prometheus-sourced refs are perishable. Retention is ⟨3 d⟩, a window not exported inside it is
gone, and every one of them gates its point at the moment the point closes. Required: M1
through M9.

CUR-sourced refs arrive late and are revised until the month closes, so none can be read at a
point's close. They gate the campaign through a single cost pass run at least 48 h after the
last point → K6. Required for the campaign: M10 through M14.

M15 through M18 are optional. M15 through M17 gate the second constraint tier and nothing else,
M18 confirms one sizing number once.
